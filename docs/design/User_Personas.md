# EduCore User Personas & Audience Profiles

## 1. Prospective Applicant (John, 16)
- **Goal**: Apply online for Senior High STEM track without visiting campus physically.
- **Pain Point**: Paper forms, lost physical documents, and opaque review status.
- **UX Expectation**: Guided 7-step wizard, auto-save draft, and real-time status tracker (`/applicant/dashboard`).

## 2. Parent / Guardian (Elena, 42)
- **Goal**: Monitor child's quarterly grades, attendance logs, and pay tuition fees online.
- **Pain Point**: Missing parent-teacher updates and unexpected fee balances.
- **UX Expectation**: Simple mobile-friendly portal (`/parent/dashboard`), multi-child switcher, and digital OR downloads.

## 3. Registrar Administrator (Marcus, 38)
- **Goal**: Audit uploaded admissions documents, schedule guidance interviews, and convert approved applicants to students.
- **Pain Point**: Disconnected applicant spreadsheets and manual student number generation.
- **UX Expectation**: Centralized workspace (`/registrar/applicants`), verification toggles, and 1-click Student Conversion.

## 4. Faculty Teacher (Dr. Maria, 45)
- **Goal**: Log daily class attendance and submit quarterly gradebooks.
- **Pain Point**: Cluttered gradebook screens and slow record saves.
- **UX Expectation**: Quick attendance sheet buttons, GPA auto-calculation, and batch gradebook submission.
