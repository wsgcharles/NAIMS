# EduCore Brand & Identity Research Analysis
**(Noah's Academy Inc.)**

## 1. Executive Summary & Brand Purpose
Noah's Academy Inc. is an institution dedicated to academic excellence, character formation, and technological readiness. EduCore functions as the official digital campus and administrative ecosystem powering the school.

## 2. Core Values & School Personality
- **Academic Rigor**: High standards, college preparedness, and STEM/ABM excellence.
- **Character & Integrity**: Responsible citizenship, ethical leadership, and accountability.
- **Inclusive Community**: Supportive atmosphere for students, parents, faculty, and administrative staff.

## 3. Visual Identity & Brand Elements
- **Emblem**: Crest with graduation cap, open book, and glowing torch.
- **Primary Color Identity**: Royal Institutional Blue (`hsl(221.2 83.2% 53.3%)`), symbolizing wisdom, trust, and authority.
- **Secondary Identity**: Deep Purple & Slate Gray, reflecting innovation and modern SaaS sophistication.
- **Tone of Voice**: Professional, welcoming, clear, encouraging, and authoritative.
